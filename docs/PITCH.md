# One-liners

**Idea statement**
> A data pipeline that turns real experts' AI-failure moments into anonymized, verifier-scored sandbox twins that frontier labs can legally train on.

**With the positioning**
> We turn real experts' AI-failure moments into anonymized, verifier-scored sandbox twins that labs can legally train on — selling the factory, not the data.

**The privacy claim (checkable, use verbatim)**
> No client file is ever uploaded or retained. What we keep — with your consent and to your credit — are anonymized synthetic cases that carry your expertise and nobody's identity.

**Why not just QEMU / why AgentENV exists**
> QEMU gets you eval speed. AgentENV exists to get environment lifecycle down to training speed. We operate at eval speed; the lab operates at training speed.

**The incentive line**
> Labs pay experts $85–200/hour to imagine tasks. Ours cost nothing, because the expert was doing their own work. The incentives finally line up.
